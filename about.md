## Our mission

- Advance fundamental and applied research in AI, automated reasoning and machine learning.
- Bridge mathematics, computer science and the natural sciences through intelligent automation.
- Transfer knowledge and technologies into education, science and industry.
- Serve the public interest by fostering open research, education and a deeper understanding of AI and its impact.

## What we do

- **Research and innovation**: basic and applied research in AI, automated reasoning and related fields. Our program covers AI for automated and interactive theorem proving, automated formalization, synthesis of mathematical objects and conjectures, and autonomous “automated mathematicians”. It builds on the founders’ work on E, Prover9, ENIGMA, MaLARea, TacticToe and CoqHammer. See [our research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}).
- **Collaboration**: international cooperation with universities, research centers and industry.
- **Education and outreach**: seminars, training programs, conferences, and open dissemination of results.
- **Open science**: tools, data and results made widely accessible.

## History

AI4REASON was registered in Prague on **24 September 2025** as an independent nonprofit research institute (*zapsaný ústav*). Its founders, Josef Urban, Cezary Kaliszyk, Stephan Schulz and Robert Veroff, have helped develop the [AITP conference](https://aitp-conference.org/) series since 2016. The name continues the [AI4REASON ERC project](https://github.com/JUrban/ai4reason-erc-project) (2015–2021) on artificial intelligence for large-scale computer-assisted reasoning.

In 2025 the institute had no employees and relied on its founders, collaborators and donors. Notable results that year were Robert Veroff’s [Prover9 proof]({{ '/veroff-aim-proof/' | relative_url }}) that commutative AIM loops are nilpotent of class 3, and the start, on 21 November 2025 and on AI4REASON servers, of low-cost autoformalization work that produced [130k lines of formal topology in two weeks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03298).

In 2026 the ERC Advanced Grant NextReason started, AI4REASON hired Thibault Gauthier, Atle Hahn, Adrian De Lon and Chad E. Brown, and the institute opened offices in the Zirkon Office Center in Karlín, opposite the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics of Charles University.

## Partners

- **[Faculty of Mathematics and Physics, Charles University](https://www.mff.cuni.cz/en)**: on 24 October 2025 we signed a statement establishing long-term collaboration in AI for mathematics, including joint research and doctoral positions, an AI4MATH study branch, seminars, workshops and student projects. Charles University is also a partner in NextReason.
- **University of Gothenburg and Chalmers University of Technology**: the University of Gothenburg coordinates NextReason. Together we organized the Gothenburg CHAIR Workshop (26–29 May 2026).
- **[SingularityNET](https://singularitynet.io/) and Ben Goertzel**: collaboration on AGI, formal reasoning and mathematics.

## Governance

The institute is managed by a **Board of Trustees** (správní rada) and a **Director**. The founders are Josef Urban, Cezary Kaliszyk, Stephan Schulz and Robert Veroff. The Board is chaired by Robert Veroff, with Cezary Kaliszyk and Stephan Schulz; Josef Urban is the Director. See [People]({{ '/people/' | relative_url }}).

We operate transparently and reinvest all resources into our mission. In 2025 no remuneration or other benefits were provided to the Director or to the members of the Board.
