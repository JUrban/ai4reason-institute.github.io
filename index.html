What we do

About the institute →

  • AI for theorem proving

    Learning-guided automated and interactive theorem proving: clause selection, premise selection, conjecturing and large proof searches.

  • Autoformalization

    Turning textbook mathematics into machine-checked libraries with LLM agents, proof checkers and automated provers, at low cost and large scale.

  • Automated mathematicians

    Synthesis of mathematical objects, programs and conjectures, and time-boxed experiments with AI agents on open problem collections.

  • Trustworthy proof

    Re-checking landmark proofs on verified foundations, explicit proof certificates, and formally verified proof checkers.

  • Collaboration and education

    Joint research and doctoral training with Charles University and the University of Gothenburg; workshops, seminars and the AITP conference.

  • Open science

    Our software, data and formal libraries are open source, and we prefer open-access publication so that others can build on our results.
