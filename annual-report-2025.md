## Establishment and mission

AI4REASON z.ú. was established in Prague on **24 September 2025** as an independent nonprofit research institute dedicated to artificial intelligence, automated reasoning and machine learning. Its mission is to combine learning with rigorous logical reasoning to advance mathematics, formal verification and trustworthy AI, and to share knowledge and tools for public benefit.

The founders are **Josef Urban, Cezary Kaliszyk, Stephan Schulz and Robert Veroff**. Josef Urban served as Director. The Executive Board (správní rada) comprised Robert Veroff as Chairman, Cezary Kaliszyk and Stephan Schulz. During the reporting period, no remuneration or other benefits were provided to the Director or to the members of the Executive Board, and there were no changes to the founding deed or to the membership of the institute’s bodies.

Registered office: V Sedlci 249/4a, 160 00 Prague 6–Sedlec, Czech Republic. Identification number: 23753161.

## Research and infrastructure

The research programme covers AI for automated and interactive theorem proving, automated formalization, synthesis of mathematical objects and conjectures, and autonomous “automated mathematicians.” It builds on the founders’ work on E Prover, Prover9, ENIGMA, MaLARea, TacticToe and CoqHammer. A notable 2025 result was Robert Veroff’s **40-day autonomous Bigloop/Prover9 run**, proving that [commutative AIM loops are nilpotent of class 3]({{ '/veroff-aim-proof/' | relative_url }}).

Groundbreaking work on low-cost autoformalization began on **21 November 2025**, using AI4REASON servers. Josef Urban reported the results in [*130k Lines of Formal Topology in Two Weeks: Simple and Cheap Autoformalization for Everyone?*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03298), submitted on **6 January 2026**. The project connects language-model agents with Chad E. Brown’s Megalodon proof checker to formalize textbook topology.

The paper reports roughly **130,000 lines** produced between 22 December 2025 and 4 January 2026 for about **USD 100** in language-model subscription costs, and approximately 160,000 lines in total by 4 January. The results span the year boundary and build on development undertaken in 2025.

## Operations and partnerships

**AI4REASON had no employees in 2025.** Its initial development relied on its founders, collaborators and external support. On 9 December 2025, the institute contracted Účto pro NNO s.r.o., represented by Petr Jun, for accounting, annual financial statements, tax-related services and payroll administration as needed.

On **24 October 2025**, AI4REASON and the **Faculty of Mathematics and Physics of Charles University** signed a statement establishing long-term collaboration in AI for Mathematics. Planned cooperation includes joint research and doctoral positions, an AI4MATH study branch, seminars, workshops and student projects.

## Funding secured in 2026

**Michael R. Douglas** donated USD 20,000 through the AI4REASON US Fund. After Myriad’s USD 1,000 management fee (5%), USD 19,000 was granted to AI4REASON for research in AI and reasoning. The grant was approved on 13 January 2026 and recorded as paid on 18 January 2026.

## Research programme in 2026

The **ERC Advanced Grant NextReason**, *Next Generation Computer Assisted Reasoning* (grant agreement 101200949), led by Josef Urban, runs from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2030. AI4REASON participates alongside the coordinating University of Gothenburg and Charles University in Prague. Its allocation is **EUR 599,057** within a total project grant of EUR 2,499,057.

In 2026, AI4REASON hired **Thibault Gauthier, Atle Hahn, Adrian De Lon and Chad E. Brown** to work on the ERC project. Joint work with Gothenburg and Charles University connects research on learning, automated reasoning and formal mathematics with doctoral training.

Operational expansion includes obtaining offices in the **Zirkon Office Center** in Karlín, Prague, opposite the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics of Charles University, supporting close collaboration with the institute’s ERC partner.

The 2026 community programme includes the **Gothenburg CHAIR Workshop** at Chalmers University of Technology (26–29 May 2026) on AI for mathematics, automated reasoning and formalization. It also includes contributions to the organization and scientific programme of **AITP’26**, the 11th Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Theorem Proving (30 August – 4 September 2026, Aussois, France), involving Robert Veroff, Stephan Schulz, Josef Urban and many international colleagues. The founders have helped develop the AITP series since 2016.

Further priorities are to advance open-source reasoning tools and datasets, reproducible experiments and autoformalization methods, and to strengthen research partnerships and doctoral education.

AI4REASON thanks its founders, donors, researchers and institutional partners for supporting its establishment and its long-term mission of independent research in AI-assisted reasoning.

## Výzkum a infrastruktura

Výzkumný program zahrnuje využití umělé inteligence při automatickém a interaktivním dokazování vět, automatizovanou formalizaci, syntézu matematických objektů a domněnek a vývoj autonomních „automatizovaných matematiků“. Navazuje na práci zakladatelů na systémech E Prover, Prover9, ENIGMA, MaLARea, TacticToe a CoqHammer. Významným výsledkem roku 2025 byl **čtyřicetidenní autonomní běh systému Bigloop/Prover9** Roberta Veroffa, který dokázal, že [komutativní AIM lupy jsou nilpotentní třídy 3]({{ '/veroff-aim-proof/' | relative_url }}).

Průlomové práce na nízkonákladové autoformalizaci začaly **21. listopadu 2025** s využitím serverů AI4REASON. Josef Urban výsledky popsal v práci [*130k Lines of Formal Topology in Two Weeks: Simple and Cheap Autoformalization for Everyone?*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03298), vložené do repozitáře arXiv dne **6. ledna 2026**. Projekt propojuje agenty založené na jazykových modelech s ověřovačem důkazů Megalodon od Chada E. Browna za účelem formalizace učebnicové topologie.

Podle této práce vzniklo v období od 22. prosince 2025 do 4. ledna 2026 přibližně **130 000 řádků** za náklady na předplatné jazykových modelů ve výši přibližně **100 USD**, přičemž do 4. ledna bylo celkem vytvořeno asi 160 000 řádků. Výsledky přesahují hranici kalendářních let a navazují na vývoj uskutečněný v roce 2025.
