---
title: Research support from SingularityNET
description: The SingularityNET Foundation provided a USD 130,000 research support grant for AGI, automated reasoning, formal verification and related research.
---
We are pleased to announce new research support from the [**SingularityNET Foundation**](https://singularitynet.io/), led by Ben Goertzel: a **USD 130,000** research support grant under an agreement dated today.

The grant supports research in artificial general intelligence, automated reasoning, autoformalization, formal verification, inference control, experiential learning, value alignment, machine ethics and related areas, while preserving the institute’s discretion over its research agenda and methods. It strengthens our mission of developing open and verifiable AI systems and advancing foundational work at the intersection of reasoning and intelligent systems.
