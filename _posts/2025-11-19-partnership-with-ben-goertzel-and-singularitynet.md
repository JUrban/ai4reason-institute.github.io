We are pleased to announce a new collaboration with [**Ben Goertzel**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Goertzel) and [**SingularityNET**](https://singularitynet.io/), to advance research at the intersection of AGI, formal reasoning and mathematics. This partnership supports our efforts in autoformalization, interpretable intelligence, and open, verifiable AI research.

As part of launching this collaboration, **AI4REASON has today received the first installment of an unrestricted gift from Ben Goertzel**, made through Novamente LLC (USD 42,000 in 2025).
