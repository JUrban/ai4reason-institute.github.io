The **Gothenburg CHAIR Workshop** at Chalmers University of Technology (26–29 May 2026) brings together researchers in AI for mathematics, automated reasoning and formalization. See the [workshop page](https://josefurban.eu/chalmers_workshop_webpage.html).

AI4REASON contributions include Zar Goertzel’s *To QED and Beyond: Adventures in Vibe Coding*, Mirek Olšák’s *Vibe-coding an ITP* and Atle Hahn’s *Autoformalization with Naproche-ZF*, along with a colloquium with local mathematicians.
