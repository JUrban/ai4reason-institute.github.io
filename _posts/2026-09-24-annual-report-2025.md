The Executive Board has approved the institute’s first **[annual report]({{ '/annual-report-2025/' | relative_url }})**, covering the period from 24 September to 31 December 2025. It is filed in Czech in the Collection of Documents; we publish both the Czech text and an English version.

Highlights:

- Founded in Prague on 24 September 2025 by Josef Urban, Cezary Kaliszyk, Stephan Schulz and Robert Veroff.
- Robert Veroff’s 40-day Bigloop/Prover9 run proved that [commutative AIM loops are nilpotent of class 3]({{ '/veroff-aim-proof/' | relative_url }}).
- Low-cost autoformalization started on AI4REASON servers on 21 November 2025, leading to [130k lines of formal topology in two weeks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03298).
- Long-term collaboration in AI for mathematics with the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics of Charles University.
- Donations of CZK 1.81 million in 2025, with no public subsidies; in 2026, the ERC Advanced Grant NextReason, a SingularityNET research grant and further gifts. See [Funding]({{ '/funding/' | relative_url }}).
- In 2026 we hired our first researchers and opened offices in the Zirkon Office Center in Karlín.
