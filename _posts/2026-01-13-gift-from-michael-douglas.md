title: Gift from Michael R. Douglas through Myriad USA
description: Michael R. Douglas donated USD 20,000 to AI4REASON through the AI4REASON US Fund at Myriad USA.
---
We are grateful to [**Michael R. Douglas**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_R._Douglas), theoretical physicist and mathematical scientist at Harvard’s Center of Mathematical Sciences and Applications, for a gift of **USD 20,000** through the AI4REASON US Fund at Myriad USA.

After Myriad’s 5% management fee, USD 19,000 was granted to AI4REASON for research in AI and reasoning; the grant was approved today. It supports our work on automated reasoning, autoformalization, and more transparent and reliable intelligent systems.
