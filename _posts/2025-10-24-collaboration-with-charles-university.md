title: Long-term collaboration with Charles University
description: AI4REASON and the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics of Charles University signed a statement establishing long-term collaboration in AI for mathematics.
---
AI4REASON and the [**Faculty of Mathematics and Physics of Charles University**](https://www.mff.cuni.cz/en) (MFF UK) have signed a statement establishing long-term collaboration in **AI for mathematics**.

Planned cooperation includes joint research and doctoral positions, an **AI4MATH** study branch, seminars, workshops and student projects. Charles University is also a partner in the ERC Advanced Grant NextReason, and since 2026 our offices are right opposite the faculty’s Karlín building.
