**[AITP’26](https://aitp-conference.org/2026/)**, the 11th Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Theorem Proving, takes place from 30 August to 4 September 2026 in Aussois, France. Robert Veroff, Stephan Schulz and Josef Urban contribute to its organization and scientific programme; the founders have helped develop the AITP series since 2016.

Contributions by AI4REASON people include:

- *Felix: a Scalable Natural Proof Assistant* (Adrian De Lon)
- *Autoformalization Experiments with Felix* and *Understandable Autoformalization* (Adrian De Lon, Atle Hahn)
- *Reinformalizing Autoformalizations: Combining Naproche and Informath* (Adrian De Lon, Aarne Ranta)
- *Program Generation as an Evolution Operator* (Thibault Gauthier)
- *Lean 4 Verified Metamath Verifier: Investigating AI-driven Software Verification* (Zar Goertzel)
- *Munkres’ General Topology Autoformalized in Isabelle/HOL* and *Autoformalizing Fast–Slow Equivalence Proofs for Atlas Computations* (with Josef Urban)
- *Neural Conjecturing for Saturation Theorem Provers* and *Teaching Vampire New Tricks* (with Josef Urban)

Abstracts, slides and links are on our [publications page]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}).
