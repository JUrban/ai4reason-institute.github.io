title: Tax-deductible giving from the US through the AI4REASON US Fund
description: Supporters in the United States can now give to AI4REASON through the AI4REASON US Fund at Myriad USA.
---
AI4REASON has established fundraising cooperation with **Myriad USA** through the **AI4REASON US Fund**, under an American Friends Fund Agreement signed today.

Our friends and supporters based in the United States can support our activities through a contribution to the AI4REASON US Fund at Myriad USA, a U.S. public charity within the meaning of Sections 501(c)(3) and 509(a)(1) of the Internal Revenue Code. Donors may be eligible for a U.S. income tax deduction for their contributions to the extent permitted by U.S. tax law. In accordance with U.S. tax law, Myriad USA retains complete control and discretion over amounts contributed to the Fund.

Donate online at [every.org/ai4reason-zu](https://www.every.org/ai4reason-zu), or see [other ways to support us]({{ '/funding/#support' | relative_url }}).
