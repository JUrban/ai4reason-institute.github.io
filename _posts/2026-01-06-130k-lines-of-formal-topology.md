Josef Urban has reported a low-cost autoformalization experiment in [*130k Lines of Formal Topology in Two Weeks: Simple and Cheap Autoformalization for Everyone?*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03298), submitted to arXiv today.

The work began on 21 November 2025 on AI4REASON servers. It connects language-model coding agents with Chad E. Brown’s **Megalodon** proof checker to formalize textbook topology from Munkres. Between 22 December 2025 and 4 January 2026 the setup produced roughly **130,000 lines** for about **USD 100** in language-model subscription costs, and approximately 160,000 lines in total by 4 January. The paper highlights the practical value of a fast checker, an initial library, and persistent repair of failed proofs.

The paper was published at [ITP 2026](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.ITP.2026.31) (LIPIcs 382). The development continues in the [Munkres general topology](https://github.com/mgwiki/mgw_test) and [algebraic topology](https://github.com/mgwiki/alg_top) Megalodon projects; see [Research]({{ '/research/#autoformalization' | relative_url }}).
