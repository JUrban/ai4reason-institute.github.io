The **ERC Advanced Grant [NextReason](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101200949)**, *Next Generation Computer Assisted Reasoning* (grant agreement 101200949), led by Josef Urban, runs from today until 31 December 2030.

AI4REASON participates alongside the coordinating **University of Gothenburg** and **Charles University** in Prague. The institute’s allocation is EUR 599,057 within a total project grant of EUR 2,499,057. The project develops logic-aware learning architectures, neuro-symbolic methods, automated formalization and autonomous reasoning systems for mathematics and formal verification.

In 2026 AI4REASON hired Thibault Gauthier, Atle Hahn, Adrian De Lon and Chad E. Brown to work on the project. Meet them on our [people page]({{ '/people/' | relative_url }}).
