Software

Systems developed or co-developed by our founders and researchers, which our projects build on.

E

A high-performance automated theorem prover for first-order logic with equality, by Stephan Schulz.

Prover9

Automated theorem prover for first-order and equational logic; Robert Veroff’s hint-based methods and Bigloop runs scale it to very long proofs.

Megalodon

Chad E. Brown’s fast proof checker for higher-order set theory, the backbone of our large autoformalization experiments.

Felix

Adrian De Lon’s natural proof assistant: controlled mathematical English in LaTeX with automated reasoning and a proof kernel.

ENIGMA and MaLARea

Learning-guided clause selection for E and machine-learning-based reasoning over large theories, from Josef Urban and colleagues.

TacticToe and CoqHammer

Learning-guided tactic proving in HOL4 (Thibault Gauthier) and hammer automation for Coq (Cezary Kaliszyk and colleagues).

Project descriptions summarize the reported state of each repository in November 2026. They distinguish checked formal artifacts from claims that still await independent review.
