Research grants

ERC Advanced Grant NextReason

Next Generation Computer Assisted Reasoning, grant agreement 101200949, led by Josef Urban, runs from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2030. AI4REASON takes part with the coordinating University of Gothenburg and Charles University. The project develops logic-aware learning architectures, neuro-symbolic methods, automated formalization and autonomous reasoning systems for mathematics and formal verification.

EUR 599,057 for AI4REASON, within a total grant of EUR 2,499,057.

SingularityNET Foundation

A research support grant from the SingularityNET Foundation, led by Ben Goertzel, under an agreement of April 2026. It supports research in artificial general intelligence, automated reasoning, formal verification and related areas, while preserving the institute’s discretion over its agenda and methods.

USD 130,000

Our sponsors

Individuals

  • Tomáš Mikolov is Chief Scientific Officer at Bottlecap AI and a leading researcher in artificial intelligence and computational linguistics, best known for pioneering work on neural language models and Word2Vec, which transformed modern natural language processing. Joint statement (PDF).
  • Ben Goertzel is a scientist, entrepreneur and philosopher recognized for his work on artificial general intelligence. Founder and CEO of SingularityNET, he advocates open, decentralized and beneficial AI development. Joint statement (PDF).
  • Michael R. Douglas is a theoretical physicist and mathematical scientist known for work in string theory, non-commutative geometry and AI for mathematics and physics. He is a Senior Research Scientist at Harvard’s Center of Mathematical Sciences and Applications.
  • Christian Szegedy is co-founder and CEO of AletheAI and a leading researcher in AI and machine learning, best known for the GoogLeNet/Inception architecture and foundational work on batch normalization and adversarial examples.

Organizations

  • SingularityNET Foundation — research support for AGI, automated reasoning, formal verification and related areas.
  • Novamente LLC — an unrestricted gift launching our collaboration with Ben Goertzel.
  • EUReason s.r.o. — a Prague technology company developing distributed, decentralized and collaborative tools for formal mathematics. Statement of support (PDF).
